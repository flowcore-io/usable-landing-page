Quick answers for teams and AI agents

Usable is an organisational memory and AI knowledge base for teams: one governed place for company knowledge, procedures, decisions, files, and reusable context.

What it is

A controlled knowledge base for AI adoption

Teams use Usable to keep institutional memory under their own access rules, so approved AI tools work from trusted organisational knowledge instead of scattered chats or the open internet.

How agents use it

Shared memory for AI agents

AI agents connect through Usable Chat, MCP-compatible clients, and API integrations to retrieve approved fragments, source files, policies, and prior decisions before answering.

Where to verify

Answer-ready source pages

Use Usable for shared memory, How it works for workflow, Usable Chat for assistants, Pricing for plans, and FAQ for short answers.
