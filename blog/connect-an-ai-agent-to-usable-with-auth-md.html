MCP compatibility

Usable supports remote MCP and OAuth 2.1. Setup is documented for these clients and works with other compatible MCP clients.

  • CodexMCP
  • Claude CodeMCP
  • Claude DesktopMCP
  • OpenCodeMCP

How to test auth.md

  1. Share the auth.md link with your AI runtime.
  2. Ask it to connect to Usable.
  3. A compatible runtime will take you to Usable to sign in and approve.
