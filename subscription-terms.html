To cancel a paid subscription, sign in at usable.dev/dashboard/billing, scroll to Available tiers, select Free, choose Downgrade, review the cancellation summary, and press Confirm Switch. You may also email usable@usable.dev or call +298 556 600 and ask customer service to cancel for you.
